# Demo Video Script — "Claim a token with your AI agent" (~2 minutes)

Goal: a client watches this and understands the whole flow in one viewing.
Record in two parts: **(A)** laptop screen — the demo page; **(B)** phone
screen — the Binance app agent chat (use the real ORYN live flow or a
testnet rehearsal; never show any seed phrase, key, or full wallet backup
screen — there is none in this flow, which is the point).

## Shot list

| # | Time | Screen | Action | On-screen text / voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:10 | Laptop | Open the demo page, scroll slowly | "Your holders don't connect a wallet. They copy one prompt." |
| 2 | 0:10–0:25 | Laptop | Click **Copy claim prompt**; toast appears | "One click. The prompt pins YOUR official contract addresses." |
| 3 | 0:25–0:40 | Phone | Open Binance app → Agentic Wallet (Developer Mode ON), paste the prompt, send | "Paste it to the agent. That's the whole integration." |
| 4 | 0:40–1:05 | Phone | Agent replies: Agent ID, mint status, claim amounts | "The agent reads the contract first — round, price, already-claimed — before touching anything." |
| 5 | 1:05–1:30 | Phone | Agent shows the **preview** of claimFree(); user types **confirm** | "Nothing executes without a preview and an explicit confirm." |
| 6 | 1:30–1:45 | Phone/Laptop | Agent reports txHash; open it on BscScan | "Done. Verifiable on-chain, like every step." |
| 7 | 1:45–2:00 | Laptop | Back to the page; show the Buy prompt panel | "Same flow for paid lots — with referrals. Page + prompts from $250. DM to launch." |

## Recording notes

- Rehearse once with sound off; captions carry the video (most clients watch muted).
- Add captions as burned-in text, large and short (the table above is the caption script).
- Blur nothing except: notification popups, other apps' balances if visible.
- End card (2 sec): "Agent-ID Claim & Mint Toolkit — oryn.family is live proof — DMs open".
- Export: 1080p MP4, under 50 MB, post natively on X (not a link) + pin the
  offer post from PITCH.md under it.
